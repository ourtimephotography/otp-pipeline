A place that feels like you

Browse our Session Locations for spots we love, or bring us somewhere that means something to you. Every session is outdoors.

Any time of day

Sunrise and sunset have beautiful, soft light, but we can make any time of day work. We’ll find what fits your schedule on your planning call.

What to wear · Families

Coordinate, don’t match

Pick two or three colors plus a neutral or two, and let everyone mix them. Earthy, muted tones and soft textures look beautiful outdoors and keep the focus on faces. Here’s how real Our Time families did it.

Dress for the ground. We often walk on grass, trails or sand, so wear shoes you can move in. Little ones: pack a backup outfit, just in case. Cooler months: sweaters, scarves and hats look great and keep everyone happy.
What to wear · Seniors

Bring two or three outfits

One a little dressier, one that’s everyday you, and one with your thing. Solid colors and simple patterns keep the focus on you, and clothes that fit well (and are steamed or ironed) make a real difference.

Changing on site is easy. Many spots have no restrooms, so we bring a pop-up changing tent. Bring shoes for every outfit, plus something comfy to walk in between spots.
What to bring

A small bag of helpers

  • Water, and an easy, non-messy snack for little ones
  • A favorite small toy or song to win over a shy toddler
  • A hairbrush, hair ties and a lint roller
  • Bringing the dog? We love that. Bring a leash, treats and a helper who can hold them between shots.
Not sure about an outfit? Send us a photo before your session and we’ll happily weigh in.
On the day

Relax, we’ve got you

  • Arrive a few minutes early so nobody feels rushed.
  • We guide every pose, then give you space to just be together.
  • Kids don’t need to sit still. Giggles, wiggles and a little chaos make great photos.
  • Phones are welcome between shots. During posed photos we’ll ask everyone to tuck them away so all eyes are on our camera.
Weather and the unexpected

Plans change, and that’s okay

Weather

We watch the forecast and will reach out by the morning of the day before if it looks like rain or storms. If we move your session, we’ll find the next date that works for both of us, at no cost.

Feeling sick?

Just let us know before the session and we’ll find a new date.

Running late?

Send us a quick message and we’ll make the most of the time we have.
