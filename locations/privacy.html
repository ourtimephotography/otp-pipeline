Privacy

Your privacy

This location library is here to help you pick a spot for your session. We keep what we learn about visitors to a minimum, and we never sell it.

What this site collects

There are no accounts or sign-ups here. We use Google Analytics to see how the library gets used, for example which locations people look at most, so we know which spots to photograph and describe next. Google Analytics uses cookies and records things like:

  • the pages and locations you view, and for how long
  • your device, browser and approximate area (city level, not your exact location)
  • how you found the site, such as a search or a link we sent

We only see these numbers as totals and trends, not as reports on any one person.

Other services the pages use

The maps come from Apple Maps, the fonts from Google Fonts, and GitHub Pages hosts the site. Like any website, these services receive basic technical details when your browser loads them, such as your IP address. Each one follows its own privacy policy.

When you contact or book with us

If you email us or fill out our booking form, we use what you send only to plan, schedule and deliver your session. We don't sell it or use it for anything unrelated to your session.

Your choices

  • You can block or clear cookies in your browser settings. The library works fine without them.
  • Google offers a browser add-on that turns off Google Analytics on every site.
  • Have a question, or want us to delete information you've sent us? Email info@ourtimephoto.com.

Kids

This site is written for parents and seniors planning a session. It isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Last updated November 10, 2026. If we change how we handle information, we'll update this page.
