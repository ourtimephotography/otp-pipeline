How to order prints

Ordering prints from your gallery takes about five minutes. Follow the pictures below. The button to tap is always circled in gold.

Are you on a phone or a computer?

Step by step

Order a print

  1. Open your gallery

    Tap the gallery link in the email we sent you. Type the password from that email, then tap Let Me In.

    Tick Remember Me so you only type the password once on this device.

  2. Tap a photo you love

    Scroll through your gallery and tap the photo you want to print. It opens big, so you can take a closer look.

  3. Tap Buy Photo

    It’s the gold button at the bottom of your screen.

  4. Tap Paper Prints

    A list of everything you can order opens. For regular photo prints, tap Paper Prints.

    Want a canvas or a framed print? Tap Wall Art instead. The steps after that work the same way.

  5. Tap Standard Sizes

    These are the sizes that fit the frames you find in stores.

  6. Pick a size

    Tap the size you want. We picked 5×7 in these pictures.

    4×6photo albums5×7a frame on a desk8×10a shelf or a small wall spot11×14 and upthe wall

    Each size shows its price right here. We blurred the prices in these pictures; your gallery shows your prices.

  7. Pick a paper

    Tap the paper you’d like. Not sure? Choose Lustre. It has a soft sheen and doesn’t show fingerprints.

    The paper names in your gallery may look a little different from these pictures. That’s OK.

  8. Check how it fits

    Prints come in set shapes, so the shaded edges get trimmed off. Drag the photo or its corners to change what’s kept.

    When it looks right, tap Continue.

    The round Our Time logo on the photo will not be on your print.

  9. Choose how many, then tap Add to Cart

    Tap + to order more than one copy. Then tap Add to Cart.

  10. Keep shopping or go to your cart

    Want more prints? Tap Continue Shopping and repeat these steps for the next photo. All done? Tap View Cart.

  11. Tap Begin Checkout

    Your cart lists everything you picked. Check it over, then tap Begin Checkout.

    The Add a digital copy box is optional. Leave it empty if you only want prints.

  12. Add your address and pay

    Type the address where the prints should go, pick a shipping speed, then add your card. You’ll see your whole order one more time before you place it.

    Checkout is run by SmugMug, the secure service that hosts our galleries. Your card details go to them, not to us.

That’s it! A professional photo lab prints your order and mails it to your door. SmugMug emails you a receipt right away.

Shortcut

Ordering lots of photos?

You can pick several photos at once and choose a print size for all of them.

  1. Tap Select photos to buy

    It’s the gold button near the top of your gallery.

  2. Tap the circle on each photo

    A check mark shows on every photo you pick. Then tap Choose a Product and carry on from step “Tap Paper Prints” above.

Good to know

A few quick answers

Will the logo be on my prints?

No. The round logo only shows on the photos in your online gallery. Your prints come out clean.

How much do prints cost?

You’ll see the price of every size and paper in your gallery before you add it to your cart. (We blurred the prices in the pictures on this page.)

Who makes the prints?

A professional photo lab, the same kind we use for our own prints. They’re made on real photo paper, so the colors match your gallery.
