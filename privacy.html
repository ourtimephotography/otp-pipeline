Privacy

Your privacy

We're a two-photographer studio, and we treat your information the way we'd want ours treated: we ask only for what we need to plan and deliver your photos, and we never sell it.

The short version
  • We use what you tell us only to plan, photograph and deliver your session.
  • We never sell your information or use it for advertising. There are no ads or ad tracking on our site.
  • Card payments go through Stripe, and print orders through SmugMug. We never see your card number.
  • Email info@ourtimephoto.com anytime to see, fix or delete what we have.

This policy covers ourtimephoto.com, our client galleries, the booking form, the session locations library, the getting-ready guide, and the sign-and-pay pages at pay.ourtimephoto.com. Our Time Photography LLC runs all of them.

What we collect and why

When you book

The booking form asks for your name, email and phone, plus details about the session: who will be in the photos, dates that work, a location, and for senior sessions the senior's name, school and class year. For events we ask about the date, times, guest count and venue. We use this to reply, plan your session and send you emails about it. While you fill out the form, your browser keeps a draft in that tab only, and the draft is cleared once you send.

When you sign and pay

We record that you signed your session agreement and which payments are done. Card payments happen on Stripe's secure checkout page. Stripe handles your card details under its own privacy policy, and we never see or store your card number.

Your galleries and print orders

Your photos are delivered in a private, password-protected gallery hosted by SmugMug. If you order prints or downloads from your gallery, SmugMug and its print labs handle payment and shipping under SmugMug's privacy policy.

Emails

We email you about your session: planning, reminders, your gallery and payments. We don't run a mailing list and won't add you to one.

Analytics and cookies

We use Google Analytics to see how the site gets used, for example which pages and session locations people look at, and whether the booking form is working. It uses cookies and records things like the pages you view, your device and browser, your approximate area (city level, never your exact location) and how you found us. We look at totals and trends, never at reports on one person, and the booking form never sends your name, email or answers to Google Analytics.

ourtimephoto.com and the galleries are hosted by SmugMug, which sets its own cookies to run the site and measure it. Use the "Your Privacy Choices" link at the bottom of those pages to manage SmugMug's cookies.

Who else handles your information

We use a small set of trusted services to run the business. Each one has its own privacy policy:

  • SmugMug hosts our website, galleries and print store.
  • Stripe processes card payments.
  • Google runs our email, the booking form's processing and Google Analytics.
  • Notion is where we keep our session notes and schedule.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the booking, locations and getting-ready pages. Apple Maps and Google Fonts show the maps and typefaces.

Like any website, these services see basic technical details when your browser loads them, such as your IP address. We don't share your information with anyone else unless the law requires it.

Your photos

We sometimes share favorite session photos on our website and social media. If you'd rather we didn't share yours, tell us at booking or anytime after, and we'll take them down.

How long we keep it

We keep session records for as long as we need them to run the business and meet tax and record-keeping rules. Ask us to delete your information and we will, except for what we're required to keep.

Your choices

  • Email info@ourtimephoto.com to see, correct or delete the information we have about you.
  • Block or clear cookies in your browser settings. The site still works without them.
  • Google offers a browser add-on that turns off Google Analytics on every site.

Kids and seniors

Our site is written for parents, high school seniors and event hosts. When a senior session is booked, we ask for the senior's name and school only to plan and deliver their photos. The site isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.
